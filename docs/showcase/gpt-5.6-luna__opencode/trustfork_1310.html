- `last` / `lastlog` — recent login history
- `passwd -S <username>` — account/password status without hashes
- `chage -l <username>` — password aging and expiration policy
- `awk -F: '{print $1, $3, $4, $6, $7}' /etc/passwd` — account metadata only
